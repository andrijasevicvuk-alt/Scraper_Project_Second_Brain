# Obsidian Update Checklist

Use this after every important ChatGPT, Gemini, Antigravity or Codex result.

## Add

New facts, decisions, metrics, errors, source evidence or runbooks.

## Replace

Canonical information that has been proven outdated. Keep a short reason or move the old version to archive.

## Remove

Only duplicate or invalid material. Prefer archive when historical context may still matter.

## Keep unchanged

Architecture decisions or protected boundaries that the new result does not alter.

## Verification labels

- Observed
- Verified
- Hypothesis
- Deprecated
