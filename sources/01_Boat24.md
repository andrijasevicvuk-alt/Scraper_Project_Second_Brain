# Source — Boat24

Boat24 is the first planned vertical slice and broad marketplace backbone for the scraper platform.

Business scope, included categories and required YPI source-level fields must be finalized in Step 4 before protected implementation.

## Discovery prototype

### Hypotheses

- Motorboat and sailboat category entry points expose discovery pages.
- Pagination may use a `page` query parameter.
- Broad searches may require partitioning to avoid a result ceiling.

### Prototype decisions

- Primary identity: stable source listing key when verified.
- Fallback identity: deterministic hash of normalized canonical detail URL with reduced confidence.
- Partition prototype: test category, price and year bounds only after pagination behaviour is measured.
- Routine starting policy: weekly light discovery with conditional detail fetches, consistent with [[06_Routine_Scrape]].

No exact partitioning rule is production-approved yet.
